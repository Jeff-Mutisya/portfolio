## The Challenge

Community spaces had no affordable internet. Any solution had to survive
power cuts, work within tight hardware budgets, and keep running after
the deployment team left — which meant documentation and local capacity
were as important as the network design itself.

## What I Did

### Site design & deployment

Designed and deployed outdoor Wi-Fi zones on MikroTik and Ubiquiti
gear: mast-mounted access points, spectrum-aware channel plans, and
bandwidth management so schools and community users each get a fair
share.

### Operations & monitoring

Set up monitoring and performance baselines, handled L2 escalations, and
wrote the runbooks local teams used for first-line troubleshooting.

### Sustainable handover

Worked with community stakeholders on sustainable operating models —
simple status dashboards, escalation paths and maintenance checklists —
so networks stayed alive beyond the project.

## Outcome

- 600+ daily users across community sites in three countries
- Networks running in Kenya, Nigeria and Malawi with local first-line support
- Reusable deployment and documentation playbooks for future sites
