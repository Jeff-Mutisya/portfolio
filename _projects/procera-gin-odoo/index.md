## The Challenge

Procera Gin was running production, inventory and distribution on
spreadsheets and disconnected tools. Orders moved through more than 20
manual touchpoints, stock levels were only accurate at month-end, and
management had no single view of the business.

## What I Did

### Discovery & process mapping

Ran structured interviews with production, warehouse, finance and
distribution teams. Mapped the full order-to-distribution workflow and
identified where handoffs failed and records were duplicated.

### System design & configuration

Configured Odoo across manufacturing, inventory, purchasing, sales and
accounting. Set up multi-step routes so goods move through the system the
same way they move through the building, and aligned tax and journal
structures with the finance team.

### Data migration

Cleaned and loaded item masters, opening stock, supplier and customer
records, with validation counts agreed with each department before
cutover.

### Training & go-live

Ran role-based training for warehouse and office users, supported users
through the first stock takes and closing cycles, and stayed on for
post-go-live refinements.

## Outcome

- One connected system replacing spreadsheets across the organisation
- Order-to-distribution flow mapped and digitised across 20+ touchpoints
- Accurate, always-on stock visibility for production and sales teams
- Users adopted the system through role-based training and floor support
