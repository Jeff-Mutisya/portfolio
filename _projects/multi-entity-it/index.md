## The Challenge

Five group entities, each with its own users, vendors and habits — but
one IT team. Accounts drifted out of alignment, telephony was a mix of
platforms, and support requests arrived through every channel except the
right one.

## What I Did

### Unified identity & Microsoft 365

Administered Microsoft 365 across all entities: licence hygiene, shared
mailbox structure, and access policies aligned with IT security
compliance requirements.

### VoIP consolidation

Managed 3CX, Teams Calling and Zoom Phone: call flows, DID assignment,
user provisioning and vendor escalation — so telephony stopped being a
per-office mystery.

### Infrastructure & support

Ran L1/L2 support, LAN/WLAN and RDP operations, and supported the Odoo
ERP instance — logging incidents properly and feeding recurring issues
back into training and process fixes.

## Outcome

- Consistent IT operations across 5 entities
- Predictable VoIP experience with clear ownership per platform
- Recurring issues reduced through incident analysis and user training
- Security compliance maintained across the group
