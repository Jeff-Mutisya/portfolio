## The Challenge

NatureLock Foods sold through multiple channels while running a
multi-entity Odoo setup. Each sales channel recorded orders differently,
the warehouse worked from printouts, and finance had to reconcile
discrepancies by hand at every close.

## What I Did

### Workflow documentation

Mapped every step of the order-to-distribution journey — more than 20
touchpoints — from quote to delivery note, and flagged each point where
data was re-keyed or lost.

### Gap analysis

Compared the mapped workflow against the existing Odoo configuration and
identified integration gaps: unlinked sales channels, mismatched units of
measure, and manual journals created to paper over sync failures.

### Fixes & automation

Closed the gaps inside Odoo: standardised product and UoM data, automated
order confirmation and delivery flows, and introduced API-based
integrations so records flow between systems without re-keying.

## Outcome

- A single documented workflow replacing tribal knowledge
- Fewer manual journals and reconciliation errors at month-end close
- Consistent product and inventory data across entities
- Warehouse and finance teams working from the same live records
